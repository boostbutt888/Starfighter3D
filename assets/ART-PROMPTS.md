## Surface atlas prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production 3D game material atlas, square 2048x2048 image, exact 2 by 2 equal quadrant layout, no gaps or border. Each quadrant is a seamless flat orthographic albedo texture lit evenly without shadows or perspective. TOP LEFT: weathered warm off-white sci-fi starfighter hull plating, fine rectangular panel joints, fasteners, minute paint chips and exhaust grime, realistic practical-film miniature surface, no text or colored insignia. TOP RIGHT: weathered blue-gray gunmetal Imperial spacecraft panels, machined small access panels, screw heads, subtle scratches, very fine dense mechanical details, no big obvious shapes. BOTTOM LEFT: dark copper-brown aged sail-barge hull cladding, long horizontal overlapping thin bronze metal strips, riveted seams, tarnish, sand abrasion, realistic distressed shipbuilding surface. BOTTOM RIGHT: muted burnt-sienna coarse woven sail cloth, fine weave, stitched reinforced seams, irregular sun fading and subtle weathering. All four square quadrants filled edge to edge, neutral diffuse illumination for use as texture maps, high frequency detail, physically believable materials. No words, no labels, no watermark, no spaceships or scene renders.

## Explosion atlas prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: VFX explosion sprite sheet for a 3D space combat game. Create one square 2048x2048 RGBA image, genuine transparent background. Exact uniform 4x4 grid of 16 equal square animation cells, no lines and no text, all cells centered consistently, no flames or smoke crosses a cell boundary. Left to right and then top to bottom is chronological progression of ONE realistic explosive detonation. Frames 1-3: sudden white hot flash, violent orange jets and small dark fragments. Frames 4-8: asymmetrical rapidly billowing turbulent orange and yellow fire engulfed in dense dark smoke with small sparks; think a practical cinema pyrotechnic explosion filmed on a high-speed camera, not a smooth energy bubble or cartoon. Frames 9-12: fire cools into scattered bright burning pockets inside expanding charcoal gray smoke. Frames 13-16: drifting soft thick smoke and glowing embers, dissipating nearly transparent in final frame. Keep each cell isolated with 8 percent transparent padding around the explosion maximum extent, no bright matte edges. Front view with detailed volumetric turbulent cauliflower lobes and wispy smoke; varied organic irregular silhouette. The evolving explosion is centered at same point in each cell; maximum expansion frame8, smoke gradually expands until frame12 and then fades. No planet, floor, background, buildings, ring shapes, force fields, graphic outlines, captions or watermark.

The requested resolution was 2048 square; the tool returned the 1254-square assets listed above. No film frames, third-party ship meshes or film sound recordings were used to produce these assets.
