PLAY WITHOUT THE BROWSER BARS

Safari on iPhone may not allow a game to enter full screen from a button. Open Starfighter as a Home Screen web app instead.

  1. Open Safari’s Share menu (or … → Share).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.
  3. Open the new Starfighter icon and turn your phone sideways.
